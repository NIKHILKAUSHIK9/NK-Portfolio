I'm a first-year M.Sc. Physics student at the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee, working at the intersection of computational astrophysics and AI/ML. I did my undergraduate in Physics (Honours) with a Computer Science minor at ARSD College, University of Delhi.

04 / scientific projects

04. Scientific Projects

Open Cluster Mass Pipeline

Python pipeline replicating the Almeida et al. (2023) methodology for batch photometry and kinematic analysis of young clusters. Computes the present-day mass function slope Γ with Poisson errors, using PARSEC isochrones, a Chabrier IMF, Monte Carlo nearest-neighbour matching in Gaia eDR3 magnitude space, binary fraction, white-dwarf contribution via the Cummings et al. (2018) IFMR, and Cardelli-law extinction.

PythonGaia eDR3Streamlit

Gaia DR3 Cluster Kinematics

Kinematic filtering, isochrone fitting and parameter derivation for the Alessi 3 and Melotte 111 clusters, with automated stellar-parameter calculation and vector-point-diagram generation.
